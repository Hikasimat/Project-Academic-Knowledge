Governance & Ethics

The 8 Non-Negotiable Standards of Commercial Conduct

These foundational principles are non-negotiable for all sales representatives, BDMs, and researchers representing Plus UAE and Vortexen in the UAE and Saudi Arabia.

🤝
Politeness & Trust

Ask respectfully, listen carefully, and build enduring executive relationships. High-pressure sales tactics are strictly forbidden.

✓
Truth & Evidence

Zero tolerance for lies, fabricated references, or unapproved authority relationships. Match every claim to documented facts.

⏱
Keep Commitments

Promise only what delivery teams can verifiably deliver. Call back promptly at promised times without exception.

📧
Document Everything

Make the phone call, follow up immediately with an email recap of agreed points, and log every interaction in CRM.

☎
Speed to Lead

Attempt qualified inbound contact within 20 minutes of capture, aiming immediately for high-intent inquiries.

🛡
Protect the Buyer

Respect client privacy, opt-outs, reasonable working hours, and explicit consent. Never spam or harass prospects.

📈
Results Over Volume

Qualified meetings held, profit-safe closed orders, and timely cash collections beat vanity activity counts.

🔁
Follow-up Discipline

A prospect reply permanently changes the workflow. Never send automated sequences to an active conversation.

⚡
Mandatory 20-Minute Inbound Lead Protocol:

Assign incoming leads and attempt first meaningful human contact within 20 minutes of capture. Prioritize phone calls where permitted; if unavailable, send a professional email acknowledgment offering the next callback time. Every important phone or WhatsApp discussion must be recapped in email and permanently logged in CRM.

CRM operating rules

Required for every lead: company, exact contact, role, source URL, country, business unit, relevant service, signal, scoring, last activity, stage, owner, next action, deadline, email thread link and suppression status.

Follow-up example: initial email → 7-day research-backed follow-up → 14-day final follow-up (no more than two; stop after reply/opt-out). Hot replies need a same-day owner and meeting proposal.

Channel discipline: calls build rapport and discover needs. Email is the formal, searchable record of quotations, commitments, scope changes, deadlines and meeting minutes. CRM logs ALL meaningful actions, including calls, LinkedIn, WhatsApp and event discussions.

Evaluation: timeliness, data completeness, next-action accuracy, meeting quality, pipeline movement, margin and cash outcomes. Do not reward meaningless email volumes.

Cross-selling without confusing the client

Rule: ask permission before introducing the sister-company service. Explain clearly which legal entity will quote, contract, invoice and deliver. Share information internally only with appropriate access and client permissions. Cross-selling is a helpful adjacent solution, not pressure.

Worldwide events, exhibitions and associations

A sales event is a researched lead source, not merely attendance. For each: target 30 qualified exhibitors → identify 10 decision-makers → schedule meetings → log qualified outcomes. Dates marked “Check” must be confirmed on organizer websites before travel or invitations.
